# Endless Climber

**Endless Climber** is an endless runner/climber game built with Phaser 3.60.0. Players climb through progressively harder levels, collecting coins to increase their score while avoiding or defeating enemies and bosses.

## Overview

A continuous climbing experience where the player ascends through infinite levels. The core loop involves navigating obstacles, collecting resources, and progressing through increasingly challenging stages.

## Key Features

- **Infinite Progression**: Continuous climbing with ever-increasing difficulty
- **Resource Collection**: Gather coins to boost your score
- **Enemies & Bosses**: Avoid regular enemies and defeat special boss encounters (e.g., Ransomware, DATALOSS)
- **Shop System**: Purchase upgrades and enhancements during gameplay
- **Health & Shield**: Manage health points and use a protective shield
- **Dynamic Audio**: Music tracks that change based on game sections (normal play, boss themes, special events)
- **Visual Style**: Minimalist dark aesthetic with a distinctive color scheme (#020617)

## Gameplay

The game combines climbing mechanics with combat elements. Players navigate through levels filled with platforms, collect coins, and face challenges from various enemy types. Boss encounters add strategic depth, requiring careful navigation and timing.

## Technical Details

- Engine: Phaser 3.60.0
- Genre: Endless Runner / Climber
- Theme: Dark, minimalistic design
